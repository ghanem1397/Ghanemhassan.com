Skin of Silence

Skin of Silence

Available

2024

A grid fractured by a diagonal wound — each cell holding its own quiet weight. This work began as a question about what we carry across borders, what survives displacement, and what hardens into silence. The single white spark at the intersection is not hope. It is the memory of choosing to continue.

The Weight
