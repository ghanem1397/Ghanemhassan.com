The Journey

Artist Evolution

Finance. Borders. Canvas.

Before

Two master's degrees.
A different language.

Finance is its own kind of architecture. Ghanem Hassan spent years building in that language — systems, structures, the logic of numbers. There is precision in it. There is also a kind of silence.

The canvas was always there. Not as escape — as recognition.

The Crossing

Lebanon to Milan.
What the body carries.

Migration is not a single event. It happens every day — in the way you move through a city that is not yours yet, in the language you reach for and miss, in the memory of light that doesn't arrive here in the same way.

His early works are heavy. Dense with what was brought across. You can feel the weight of them before you understand what they hold.

The Practice

Built, not painted.
Layer by layer.

The surfaces Hassan works with are not painted in the conventional sense. They are built. Materials applied, pressed, removed, rebuilt. Each layer is a decision. Each texture is a year.

Influenced by Kandinsky's faith in emotional abstraction and Pollock's physical commitment — but finding a path that belongs entirely to the specific experience of someone who has lived across cultures, who knows what it costs to rebuild a self in a new language.

Now

Milan. Gallery.
Still building.

Permanent presence at Passepartout Gallery. International exhibitions. Collaboration with Sartorio. A body of work that is still growing — deeper, slower, more certain of what it wants to say.

The evolution is ongoing. Each new canvas is a continuation of the same question: what survives? What is made from what survives?
