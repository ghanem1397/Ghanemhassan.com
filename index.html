The Material

Diamonds. Emeralds.
Pressed into the surface.

These are not decorative elements. In Hassan's work, precious stones are embedded directly into the canvas — representing endurance under pressure, the crystallization of lived experience, and the value that only emerges through fracture. Each stone is placed by hand. Each placement is a decision.

◆

Conflict becomes crystal.
Pressure becomes permanence.

The Artist

Born in Lebanon.
Built in Milan.

Ghanem Hassan left finance — two master's degrees, a different life — and returned to what the body already knew. His canvases are not painted. They are built. Layer by layer, by hand, until the surface carries the same weight as the memory. Diamonds and emeralds embedded throughout — pressure transformed into permanence.
