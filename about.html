The Artist

Ghanem Hassan

Lebanese · b. 1993 · Based in Milan, Italy

Ghanem Hassan is a Lebanese contemporary abstract artist based in Milan whose work explores resilience, memory, and transformation through emotionally layered compositions.

Born in Lebanon in 1993 and shaped by a life lived across borders, his practice is rooted in personal experience — themes of departure, identity reconstruction, and what the body carries long after the mind has moved on.

After earning two master's degrees in finance, he chose art as his primary language. Not as escape — as recognition. The canvas was always there.

Working through abstraction, Hassan creates surfaces of extraordinary physical depth: layers of material applied, pressed, and rebuilt by hand. Into this, he embeds diamonds and emeralds directly — not as decoration, but as symbol. These precious stones represent endurance under pressure, the crystallization of lived experience, and the value that only emerges through fracture.

His practice is influenced by Kandinsky's faith in emotional abstraction and Pollock's physical commitment to the canvas — yet remains entirely his own. Each work is a record of time, pressure, and survival.

His work has been exhibited internationally across Europe, the United States, and Asia — including Milan, New York, London, Hong Kong, Athens, and Brussels. He is a permanent artist at Passepartout Gallery, Milan, and has collaborated with luxury fashion brand Sartorio.

Hassan is available for collector inquiries, commissions, gallery collaborations, and curatorial projects.
