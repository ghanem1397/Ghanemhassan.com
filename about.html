The Artist

Ghanem Hassan

Lebanese · Milan · Contemporary Art

Ghanem Hassan is a Lebanese contemporary artist and curator based in Milan.

Born in Lebanon and shaped by a life lived across borders, his work explores what survives displacement — the memory carried in the body, the silence that forms where language fails, and the resilience that grows in the space between cultures.

Working primarily in mixed media on canvas, Hassan builds surfaces of extraordinary physical depth: layers of texture, pressure and colour applied by hand, each work demanding to be seen up close. His practice is influenced by Kandinsky's emotional abstraction and Pollock's physical engagement with the canvas — yet remains entirely his own.

His work has been exhibited internationally, including the Unfolding Visions exhibition, and he is a permanent artist at Passepartout Gallery. He has collaborated with luxury brands including Sartorio, merging art with fashion and material culture.

Before art, there were two master's degrees in finance. A different life, a different language. The decision to leave was also a painting — the first one.

Hassan is available for collector inquiries, commissions, and gallery collaborations.
